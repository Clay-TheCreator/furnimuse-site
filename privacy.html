Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

The short version. Your room photos, designs and stickers stay on your phone. Furnimuse does not ask you to create an account, does not show ads, and does not sell your personal information.

Furnimuse (“we”, “us”) is a mobile app that lets you place furniture over a photo of your room and find where to buy it. It is operated by Clay Wells, Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Information that stays on your device

  • Room photos you choose from your library or take with the camera.
  • Your designs (which pieces you placed and where), room scale settings, and design names.
  • Stickers you create from your own photos, including the names and sizes you give them.
  • Your app preferences, such as whether you have seen the welcome screens.

We do not receive or store any of this on our servers. It is saved only in the app’s storage on your device. If you back up your phone with iCloud or a computer, your backup may include this data and is governed by Apple’s terms. You can remove it at any time with Settings → Delete all my data in the app, or by deleting the app.

Permissions

  • Camera: used only when you tap Take a photo, to photograph your room.
  • Photo library: used only for the photos you choose to open in the app.
  • Clipboard: the app reads an image from your clipboard only when you tap Paste from clipboard to create a sticker.

The product catalogue

To show furniture, the app downloads product names, sizes, prices, images and links from our backend service provider, Supabase. Like any internet request, this exposes technical information, such as your IP address and device type, to that provider and its infrastructure. We do not link these requests to your identity, and no personal content from your device is sent with them.

Subscriptions

Furnimuse is offered as an auto-renewing subscription with a free trial. Purchases are processed by Apple; we never see your payment card details. We use RevenueCat, a subscription management service, to confirm whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app identifier, your purchase and subscription status, and basic device and app information. See the privacy policies of Apple and RevenueCat for how they handle data.

Links to retailers and affiliate links

When you tap Buy, the app opens the retailer’s website in your browser. The retailer’s own privacy policy then applies. Some links are affiliate links, which means the retailer or an affiliate network may use cookies or similar technology on their site to credit a purchase to us, and we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. We do not receive your personal details from these retailers.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information.
  • We do not show third-party ads or include advertising trackers.
  • We do not collect your contacts or your precise location.
  • We do not currently use analytics tools. If that changes, we will update this policy first.

Retention and deletion

Data on your device stays there until you delete it. Because we do not hold your photos or designs, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. If you contact us, we keep your email and messages only as long as needed to help you.

Children

Furnimuse is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security

Your data is protected by your device’s own security features. We use reputable providers for the catalogue and subscriptions. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we work to keep the data we handle to a minimum.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information we hold. Since we hold very little, the easiest way to exercise these rights is to email us and we will respond as required by applicable law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date at the top shows when it was last revised, and we will make significant changes visible in the app or on this page.
