Common questions

How do I make pieces look the right size?

Tap the ruler icon in the toolbar, drag the two handles along something you know the size of (a door, for example), tap Set length, and enter its real height. Pieces with a real width then appear at true scale in that photo.

How do I add my own furniture?

In Photos, press and hold a piece of furniture until it lifts out of the background, then tap Copy. In Furnimuse, tap Add, choose My stickers, and tap Paste from clipboard.

Where are my designs and photos stored?

On your phone only. Furnimuse does not upload your room photos or designs. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings in the app and tap Delete all my data. Deleting the app also removes it from your phone.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Furnimuse. For refund requests, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

A product link or price looks wrong

Prices and availability come from the retailers and can change. Please email us the product name and we will check it.
